## Product decisions from the journey review

The immediate goal is to help a seller prepare a credible listing and a buyer understand the work required to take it over. U4 removes setup interruptions, U5 makes handover facts visible, U6 preserves the buyer's place, U8 turns missing facts into seller questions and U9 explains supported capabilities. U11 records the exact deal, keeps approval history and connects purchase discussions to the payment gate. These address observed friction in the current application; they are not evidence of improved conversion yet.

| Next experiment | Why it follows these updates | Success measure |
|---|---|---|
| Observe sellers creating their first private project and publishing it | Check whether the checklist helps or adds too much reading | Time to first saved project; draft-to-publication completion; fields causing abandonment |
| Ask buyers to compare listings with completed briefs | Test whether assets, costs and setup details answer real purchase questions | Detail-to-qualified-conversation rate; missing questions raised by buyers |
| Observe buyers and sellers agreeing and revising an itemized scope | U11 is implemented locally; test whether its terms, revisions and approvals remove ambiguity without adding abandonment | Completed handovers; time to acceptance; unresolved transfer issues |
| Add dated evidence for demos, repository ownership and operating costs | Build confidence from inspectable facts | Listings with current evidence; buyer-reported confidence and corrections |

Instrument these funnel events before making numerical improvement claims. Keep mandatory listing requirements light; use optional guidance, explicit unknowns and recoverable saves. Prioritize completing and validating the payment/transfer journey before adding new AI scores or notification campaigns.

## Product priorities

Focus on small, transferable projects a buyer can actually run. Validate demand with buyers and sellers before adding large feature sets.

1. **Listing evidence:** working demo, repository ownership, build date, license, running costs and dependency inventory. Measure complete listings and qualified buyer conversations.
2. **Reliable transfer:** code/domain/assets checklist, deployment instructions and acceptance milestones. Measure successful handovers and time to transfer.
3. **Buyer matching:** saved searches by budget, stack and effort required, with opt-in alerts. Measure useful matches.
4. **Evidence-based AI:** explain missing evidence and operating risks; show assumptions behind estimates. Measure usefulness and correction rates.
